Study of the information-theoretic foundations of secure aggregation for
distributed learning, with implementations and comparisons of
secure-aggregation algorithms using groupwise keys. Experiments are
accelerated with Python parallel computing.

**Institution:** University of Utah · **Advisor:** Dr. Mingyue Ji
· **Tech:** Python, information theory
