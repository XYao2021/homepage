A-DEFEAT is a decentralized learning algorithm with an adaptive
error-feedback (error-compensation) mechanism. The work includes the key
proof technique for the adaptive mechanism, convergence analysis of the
proposed algorithm with comparisons of convergence rates against existing
methods, and empirical evaluation under a range of settings.

**Institution:** University of Utah · **Advisor:** Dr. Mingyue Ji
· **Tech:** Python, optimization theory
