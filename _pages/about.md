I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the
[University of Florida](https://www.ece.ufl.edu/), advised by Dr. Mingyue Ji.
I began my Ph.D. at the University of Utah in 2021 and moved to UF with my
advisor in 2024. Before that, I earned an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from
George Washington University and a B.Eng. in Electronic Information
Engineering from Tianjin University of Science and Technology.

I build **machine-learning systems for real-world wireless networks** — from
C++ physical-layer software to reinforcement-learning control deployed over
the air.

Research
======
My research sits where machine learning meets wireless systems.

* **Real-world wireless testbed.** I implement the physical layer in C++ —
  modulation, demodulation, filtering, synchronization, and channel
  encoding/decoding — wrapped in Python APIs for scripted over-the-air
  experiments across heterogeneous radio hardware.
* **Reinforcement learning on live networks.** I design new state
  representations for RL-based dynamic control, evaluate them across
  algorithms in simulation, and deploy them on the physical testbed.
* **Federated learning and secure aggregation.** I work on decentralized
  federated learning with error compensation and information-theoretic
  secure aggregation with groupwise keys.
* **Platforms.** I assemble UAVs with integrated wireless payloads and am
  building an AWS-hosted web interface for remote testbed access.

See the [Projects]({{ base_path }}/portfolio/) page for details on each direction, and
[Publications]({{ base_path }}/publications/) for papers.
